How to read these charts

These are observed-device counts, not visitor totals or measured occupancy. Positive-duration stops contribute to every hour they overlap; zero-duration observations contribute to the hour of their ping. Daily counts include stops spanning midnight. Devices are deduplicated within each place or time bucket; counts across buckets are not additive.

Zero-duration observations are excluded from dwell percentiles. Blank activity is rendered as zero observations; collection coverage is unknown. The hourly mean includes all calendar days between the first and last valid observations.
